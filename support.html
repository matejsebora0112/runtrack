Frequently asked questions

How do I record an activity?

Tap the yellow + button, choose your activity from the list, and hit Start. GPS activities (like running or cycling) track your route automatically; gym and yoga sessions track time only.

Why isn't my GPS route showing up?

Make sure Location access is allowed for TrackFit in your device's Settings, and that you have a clear view of the sky when starting outdoor activities.

How do food suggestions work?

After you finish an activity, TrackFit looks at the calories you burned and your goal (set in Settings) to suggest food that fits. You can change your goal anytime.

Can I use TrackFit in another language?

Yes. Go to Settings → Language to switch between Slovak, Czech and English.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us at get.trackfit.app@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and we'll take care of it.
